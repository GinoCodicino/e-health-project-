Goal of the game: The game raises awareness about climate change: it helps players examine their views with facts from the IPCC report, without lecturing them.

Fact from the IPCC report that came up in a conversation with a target player:
The higher the magnitude and the longer the duration of overshoot, the more ecosystems and societies are exposed to greater and more widespread changes in climatic impact-drivers, increasing risks for many natural and human systems. Compared to pathways without overshoot, societies would face higher risks to infrastructure, low-lying coastal settlements, and associated livelihoods. Overshooting 1.5°C will result in irreversible adverse impacts on certain ecosystems with low resilience, such as polar, mountain, and coastal ecosystems, impacted by ice-sheet melt, glacier melt, or by accelerating and higher committed sea level rise. (high confidence)

[Q2-A1-FR1] The system shall allow players to engage with climate change content that challenges their existing beliefs while presenting factual information from the IPCC report.

[Q4-A1-FR1] The system shall allow players to evaluate climate facts by accessing peer-reviewed data and simulations, ensuring accuracy and scientific rigor.

[Q10-A1-FR2] The system shall present the player with factual insights about travel's impact on the environment, encouraging them to reflect on their own role in climate change without direct instruction or lecturing.

[Q11-A1-FR2] The system shall provide a reflective moment at the summit where players can engage with the game's climate facts and consider their own impact without being lectured.
